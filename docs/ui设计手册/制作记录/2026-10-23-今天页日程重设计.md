## 最终提示词

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create the updated MissionBook Today-page design reference for a real Flutter app. Use the attached references for the warm white, forest green, pale lime, clean compact timeline style. Produce one polished landscape design board containing three tall phone screens side by side, labeled "0 条日程", "1 条日程", "12 条日程". Clear readable Chinese typography, restrained 20px rounded white sections, simple line icons, no perspective tilt, no decorative illustrations except a small empty calendar icon.
Palette exactly: background #F7F8F2, white #FFFFFF, forest green #244D3D, lime #D7F57B, secondary text #617067, hairline #DCE3D8.
Every phone: "今天" heading, date "10月23日 · 周五" with a small dropdown chevron, round green plus at upper right. Correct seven-day strip: for October 23 2026, Monday is 19: 一19 二20 三21 四22 五23 六24 日25. Select Friday 23 in rounded forest green rectangle. Bottom navigation 今天 / 目标 / 历史 / 我的, 今天 selected. No search button.
Main white section "计划日程" and count. First empty state count "共 0 项", small calendar outline, "这一天还没有固定任务", "留一点空白，按自己的节奏安排", light-green "+ 新建任务" button. Natural compact height, no dummy rows or timeline.
Second count "共 1 项", "查看全部 ›", clock caption "现在 16:54". Only one timeline row with tiny lime outlined dot at left, time "16:30", title "开发 MissionBook 页面", pale lime "当前时段" badge. Background very pale lime. Dark-green horizontal progress bar at 80%, exact caption "计划时段剩余 6 分钟", "80%", small forest-green "查看" button. NO execution/start button. This is a plan-time bar.
Third count "共 12 项", "查看全部 ›", clock caption "现在 16:54". Show four compact rows connected by fine grey vertical line and dots: 14:00 整理 Flutter 笔记 / 时段已过 / 计划 30 分钟; 16:30 开发 MissionBook 页面 / 当前时段 with same highlighted expanded progress as phone two; 18:30 复习 Flutter 动画 / 待到时 / 计划 30 分钟; 20:00 代码评审 / 待到时 / 计划 30 分钟. Footer "另有 8 项 · 查看全部 ›". Long titles can use two lines; don't shrink type. This section uses roughly 40 percent of phone height, with other sections flowing below.
All three below: white "自选时间" section with "+ 添加"; two rows with outline document icons, "阅读设计规范" and "整理项目文档", secondary "计划 60 分钟" and "计划 30 分钟". Then white "安排概览" section with three simple icon/count columns. Phone one: 0 固定日程, 2 自选任务, 90 计划分钟. Phone two: 1 固定日程, 2 自选任务, 120 计划分钟. Phone three: 12 固定日程, 2 自选任务, 450 计划分钟.
Important: this design reflects currently implemented data only. Do NOT show completed, running, paused, START, completion checkmarks, or fake completion metrics. Do NOT duplicate the old inaccurate weekdays from reference. Design board should feel like a coherent modern productivity app, compact and airy.
