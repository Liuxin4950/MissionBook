# 目标空态插画

用户要求目标页空态采用历史页的简洁风格。使用内置 image_gen 生成透明 PNG，选用小旗、纸质路径和两片叶子的纸艺造型，配合历史页已有的暖白纸纹、鼠尾草绿和居中文案。

- 正式资源为 [goal-flag.png](../../../assets/illustrations/goal-flag.png)，1402 × 1122，RGBA，包含透明通道，563,127 字节。
- 使用位置为 [GoalsPage](../../../lib/presentation/goals/list/goals_page.dart) 的空状态，复用 AppSectionCard 与 AppEmptyState。
- 源生成文件保留在本机 Codex generated_images 目录，正式界面直接读取仓库资源。
- 进行中、已搁置、已达成与已放弃分类使用同一插画；空态使用简短状态标题，删除重复说明，创建流程保留，导入目标大纲收进右上角更多菜单，其他分类保留返回进行中。

生成提示词如下。

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a small transparent-background empty-state illustration for the goals page of the MissionBook productivity app. Create one compact, centered paper-craft still life: a little muted sage-green pennant flag on a thin simple wooden pole, standing at the end of a short gently curling warm-ivory paper ribbon path, with only two small olive-green paper leaves at the base. A calm, minimal tactile paper sculpture, subtle fibrous paper texture, soft natural diffuse light and a very faint soft contact shadow. The graphic must read clearly when displayed at about 150 by 120 logical pixels. Match the feel of an elegant stationery empty-state graphic made of a few blank ivory sheets on a sage backing: restrained, airy, warm ivory, sage and olive, low contrast. One isolated object grouping, generous transparent space around it, three-quarter view, no surrounding landscape or scene. Truly transparent alpha background. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no borders, no picture frame, no sparkles, no additional decorations. Avoid tall heavy stone blocks and detailed scenic backgrounds.
```

已检查生成图的造型、配色、空白文字与透明通道。布局回归覆盖目标新增、导入、状态切换，以及窄屏大字号和宽屏。
