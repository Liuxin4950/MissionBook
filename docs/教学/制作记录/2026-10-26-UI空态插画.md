## 最终提示词

### 目标路径

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: small decorative empty-state illustration for MissionBook, a calm learning and task journal app. Primary request: create ONE refined editorial paper-craft illustration of a small open ivory notebook, a gently curved stepped paper path emerging from the pages toward a tiny muted forest-green flag. It conveys turning an intention into small actions. Style/medium: tactile matte paper, subtle soft 3D depth, quiet contemporary editorial illustration, restrained and sophisticated, no cartoon faces. Composition: one compact centered still-life, fully contained, generous clear transparent margin; useful at 180-240 logical pixels wide. Color palette: predominantly warm ivory and light stone grey, desaturated sage accents, small dark forest-green strokes, at most one tiny pale yellow-green bookmark; no large saturated shapes. Lighting: soft natural studio light, subtle contact shadows, clean edges. Background MUST be actually transparent, no backdrop, no enclosing card, no gradient rectangle, no checkerboard painted into image. Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no charts, no checkmarks, no success badges, no people, no additional objects. This illustration accompanies a create-goal button and must visually support, never overpower, the button.
```

Match a tactile matte paper illustration style, soft natural light, restrained 3D depth and subtle contact shadows. Predominantly warm ivory and light stone, muted sage, tiny forest-green detail only; quiet, airy, calm and refined, not childish. Entire composition centered and fully contained with generous transparent margin, intended for 140-180 logical pixels wide. Actual transparent background, no rectangular backdrop, no scenery. NO text, NO digits, NO weekday letters, NO grids, NO checkmarks, NO clocks, NO success badges, NO interface or buttons, NO faces, NO logo, NO watermark. This supports a task creation action without competing with it.
