## 2. 助手欢迎插画

- 使用内置 imagegen，透明背景；生成图片复制到 [实际资源](../../../assets/illustrations/assistant-notebook.png)，通过 pubspec 和 AppIllustration 接入欢迎区。
- 纸张、叶片、森林绿与已有插画保持一致；图像不承载文字或按钮。AppIllustration 按显示宽度限制解码尺寸。
- 角色入口图标沿用现有 Material 线性图标体系：灵感、书页、指南针。角色名称、定位和回答风格可配置，未引入另一套应用主题。

提示词：

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: a compact transparent welcome illustration for the Chinese productivity app MissionBook's AI assistant.
Primary request: one tasteful paper-craft vignette, a small open ivory notebook with softly rounded thick paper pages, one restrained forest green four-point inspiration spark floating just above the open pages, and a small natural olive leaf tucked at the edge. The notebook should feel useful and calm, like the existing app's paper stationery illustrations. Soft dimensional clay-and-paper rendering, subtle natural texture, gentle realistic ambient shadow directly beneath the object, no visible environment. Palette: warm ivory, muted sage, forest green #244D3D, a tiny pale lime highlight. Centered compact composition that reads well at 160 by 136 logical pixels; clean silhouette with generous transparent margin. No typography, no letters, no numbers, no interface, no robot face, no app logo, no glossy plastic, no neon, no busy ornament. Transparent background, no opaque rectangular backing. This is an illustration asset, not a button or a replacement app icon.
```
