# 04 MB书脊

生成方式：内置 image_gen；概念预览，每张同时展示 Logo 与 App 图标。

参考素材：用户剪贴板书页标记、UI 手册初稿封面、现有 v3 图标。

Use case: logo-brand. Design one polished landscape brand concept presentation for Mission Book, approx 1536x1024. Three reference images: 1 tiny original sage green open-book silhouette, PRIMARY shape reference; 2 original Mission Book cover, PRIMARY brand mood reference, warm ivory and sage, quiet literary elegant; 3 current app icon, supporting reference for open book plus completion check and forest-green background. Fuse these references thoughtfully into a coherent new original identity. This is concept exploration for the same app. Flat clean ivory background, generous negative space, impeccable restrained design. Consistent presentation layout: small concept number upper left; large standalone logo symbol on left with exact wordmark "Mission Book" beneath it in a refined readable serif; on right a large rounded square APP ICON tile containing exactly the same symbol adapted for small-screen readability. The logo and app icon MUST share the same distinctive geometry. Only text is the number and "Mission Book"; no slogans, tiny annotations or other letters. No desk or phone mockups, no stock watermarks. Main marks bold and simple, legible at 48 px. Do not reproduce cover photography.  Concept number "04". Direction 04: subtle MB monogram hidden in an open book. Use two broad sculpted page shapes and negative-space spine geometry to imply M in the open spread and a restrained B-shaped double-page curve on the right. First impression MUST be an elegant open book, letters secondary, not a typographic MB printed on a book. Thick stable geometry with softened corners. Forest green single-color logo on ivory, same ivory book-monogram on forest green app tile. Premium editorial serif wordmark, flat vector-like, no checkmark and no decorative badges.
