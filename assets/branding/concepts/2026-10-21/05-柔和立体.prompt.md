# 05 柔和立体

生成方式：内置 image_gen；概念预览，每张同时展示 Logo 与 App 图标。

参考素材：用户剪贴板书页标记、UI 手册初稿封面、现有 v3 图标。

Use case: logo-brand. Design one polished landscape brand concept presentation for Mission Book, approx 1536x1024. Three reference images: 1 tiny original sage green open-book silhouette, PRIMARY shape reference; 2 original Mission Book cover, PRIMARY brand mood reference, warm ivory and sage, quiet literary elegant; 3 current app icon, supporting reference for open book plus completion check and forest-green background. Fuse these references thoughtfully into a coherent new original identity. This is concept exploration for the same app. Flat clean ivory background, generous negative space, impeccable restrained design. Consistent presentation layout: small concept number upper left; large standalone logo symbol on left with exact wordmark "Mission Book" beneath it in a refined readable serif; on right a large rounded square APP ICON tile containing exactly the same symbol adapted for small-screen readability. The logo and app icon MUST share the same distinctive geometry. Only text is the number and "Mission Book"; no slogans, tiny annotations or other letters. No desk or phone mockups, no stock watermarks. Main marks bold and simple, legible at 48 px. Do not reproduce cover photography.  Concept number "05". Direction 05: bridge between minimal cover mark and the existing tactile app icon. Simplified upright open book with just two broad warm ivory pages, restrained sage cover edge and one small muted lime completion check integrated into the lower right page as a folded bookmark. On the left present its matching FLAT sage logo silhouette with a clean negative-space check; on the right present the SAME exact silhouette as an app tile on deep forest green with soft shallow paper relief and very subtle shadows. Mostly flat, gentle material depth, never detailed realistic pages, never thick toy-like plastic, no glossy metallic effects. Far simpler and more silhouette-faithful than reference 3.
