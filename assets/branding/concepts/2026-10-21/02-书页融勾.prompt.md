# 02 书页融勾

生成方式：内置 image_gen；概念预览，每张同时展示 Logo 与 App 图标。

参考素材：用户剪贴板书页标记、UI 手册初稿封面、现有 v3 图标。

Use case: logo-brand. Design one polished landscape brand concept presentation for Mission Book, approx 1536x1024. Three reference images: 1 tiny original sage green open-book silhouette, PRIMARY shape reference; 2 original Mission Book cover, PRIMARY brand mood reference, warm ivory and sage, quiet literary elegant; 3 current app icon, supporting reference for open book plus completion check and forest-green background. Fuse these references thoughtfully into a coherent new original identity. This is concept exploration for the same app. Flat clean ivory background, generous negative space, impeccable restrained design. Consistent presentation layout: small concept number upper left; large standalone logo symbol on left with exact wordmark "Mission Book" beneath it in a refined readable serif; on right a large rounded square APP ICON tile containing exactly the same symbol adapted for small-screen readability. The logo and app icon MUST share the same distinctive geometry. Only text is the number and "Mission Book"; no slogans, tiny annotations or other letters. No desk or phone mockups, no stock watermarks. Main marks bold and simple, legible at 48 px. Do not reproduce cover photography.  Concept number "02". Direction 02: an integrated checkmark, not a sticker. Broad upright open-book silhouette based on original shape. One continuous negative-space page-turn line in the right page resolves into a crisp subtle completion check, carved into the book's form; left page quiet and solid. Keep the check understated and structurally part of the page contour. Forest green logo on ivory; app tile forest green with ivory mark and tiny muted chartreuse at the turned-page/check tip. Elegant flat geometric vector-like design, avoid literal detailed illustrated book.
